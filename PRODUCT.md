# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Soma is primarily for consumer wellness users who want help regulating anxiety, sleep, focus, grounding, or energy through adaptive music. They may arrive in a sensitive state, with limited patience for technical setup or unclear controls. The interface should make the next step feel natural, understandable, and reassuring.

Secondary audiences may include reviewers, demo observers, and health technology stakeholders, but the default product lens is consumer comprehension and therapeutic trust.

## Product Purpose

Soma turns biometric signals, user context, and music preference inputs into personalized AI-generated therapeutic music sessions. The product should help users start a guided session, understand why a session was recommended, review outcomes, and manage personal profile or API settings without feeling overwhelmed.

The product is a wellness support tool, not a diagnostic or emergency medical device. Success means the user understands what to do next, trusts the recommendation enough to begin, and can recover from uncertainty without leaving the flow.

## Brand Personality

Immersive, poetic, and scientific. Soma should feel atmospheric and restorative while still showing enough mechanism, evidence, and state feedback to earn confidence.

The voice should be calm, concrete, and supportive. Avoid vague mystical language when the interface is asking users to make decisions.

## Anti-references

- Operations that feel unnatural or hard to understand.
- Generic AI or SaaS aesthetics where glass panels, glows, gradients, and identical cards become the design instead of supporting comprehension.
- Wellness app cliches that feel soft but lack technical credibility.
- Clinical dashboards that feel cold, intimidating, or detached from the user's emotional state.
- Overstimulating motion, visual noise, or control density that increases anxiety.

## Design Principles

- Make the next action obvious before making the interface impressive.
- Explain the therapeutic mechanism at the moment it helps confidence, not as background decoration.
- Keep the atmosphere immersive, but preserve reading clarity and emotional safety.
- Treat profile, vitals, reports, and settings as one coherent care journey, not separate feature islands.
- Reduce cognitive load for first-time users; reveal advanced controls only when they are useful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Accessibility requirements are not finalized. Until they are, audit against WCAG AA basics, reduced-motion needs, color-blind readability, visible focus states, keyboard access, and clear recovery paths for confused users.
