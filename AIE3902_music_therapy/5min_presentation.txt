================================================================================
  DELIVERY TIPS
================================================================================
  • Keep each slide to 1 headline + 3 bullets max.
  • Spend most time on Slides 3–5 (core evidence).
  • If asked about limitations: pilot size (N=30), simulated generation
    environment, no control group, single institution.
  • Common professor questions:
    Q: "Why is Cohen's d so high for a small N?"
    A: The intervention target was very specific and the VAS change was
       consistent across participants — small SD of differences amplifies d.

    Q: "Is this generalizable beyond college students?"
    A: Not yet — this study was scoped to 18–22 undergrad profiles. Older
       adults or clinical populations need separate validation.

    Q: "What about placebo effect?"
    A: Apple Watch biofeedback provides an objective cross-check. The
       convergent physiological signal reduces but doesn't eliminate it.
       A sham-music control arm is the key next step.
